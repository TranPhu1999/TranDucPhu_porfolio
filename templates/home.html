AI / Research

The Temporal Architecture of Video Conversion

Treats short-form advertising videos as sequences of timestamped events instead of static content. A two-tier pipeline asks “what sound is present?” (an AST classifier whose 527 AudioSet classes are mapped to marketing node types) and “what production decision was made?” (signal processing for drops, risers, silence gaps, tempo changes, speech and spoken keywords). Per-batch results are merged into one chronological event sequence per video for downstream analysis.

  • Python
  • AST
  • WhisperX
  • Librosa
  • Demucs
  • Pyannote

AI / Research

Text-to-Video Retrieval · AI Challenge

Team HCMUS-NLPS entry for a text-to-video retrieval challenge: type a sentence, get back the matching clips. Sampled frames are encoded with CLIP4Clip (CLIP ViT-B/32) into a Milvus vector index, text queries are embedded the same way and answered by similarity search, with a Gradio demo on MSR-VTT test videos.

Snake with Deep Q-Learning

A Pygame Snake game and a DQN agent that learns it from scratch. The state is 11 booleans (danger ahead/left/right, current direction, food direction), a small PyTorch Q-network picks the move, and training mixes short-term updates with experience replay.

Classic ML & algorithms

Wordle Solver

An information-theory Wordle solver: each guess is the word that maximises expected entropy over the remaining candidates, checked against a Wordle API. Version 2 caches a pre-computed pattern matrix in NumPy, which in the notebook cuts one solve from 1 min 28 s to 4.27 s.

  • Python
  • NumPy
  • Information theory
